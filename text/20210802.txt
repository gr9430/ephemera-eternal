Orlando punk trio Vicious Dreams celebrate album release at Uncle Lou’s with Wet Nurse
Punk trio Vicious Dreams were finally able to throw a record release party for their 2020 self-titled LP, and they did it in high Orlando underground style. Opening were local legends Wet Nurse and Dougie Flesh & the Slashers and the show went down in the hallowed halls of Uncle Lous. Much sweaty celebration ensued. Hope we can keep having shows like this happen. Jim Leatherman got the birds-eye view.
