Cold Creek Manor Length: 1 hour, 58 minutes Studio: Touchstone Pictures Website: http://coldcreekmanor.movies.go.com/ Release Date: 2003-09-19 Cast: Dennis Quaid, Sharon Stone, Stephen Dorff, Juliette Lewis, Christopher Plummer Director: Mike Figgis Screenwriter: Richard Jefferies Music Score: Mike Figgis WorkNameSort: Cold Creek Manor Our Rating: 3.00
Gentrification ignites murderous envy in Mike Figgis’ mediocre thriller, in which a Noo Yawk family seeking safety purchases a country home Ð only to be terrorized by its resentful former owner (Stephen Dorff). Unfortunately, Figgis’ surface-level potboiler would rather pursue hit-and-miss scares than explore cleverer conceits like its promising class-war subtext. It’s fun, though, to see the valiant mom played by a totally out-of-her-element Sharon Stone, whose every glance conveys the contradictory desire to cut loose and party with Dorff.
