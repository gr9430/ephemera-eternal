Hardcore supergroup Ascend/Descend are ready to riot tonight at Uncle Lou’s
Tonight’s a good of a night as any for some sonic catharsis. To that end, Boston hardcore supergroup Ascend/Descend (featuring mem. of American Nightmare, Lunglust, Math the Band) are a can’t-miss live prospect. Pure fury. Time to scream.
Ascend/Descend play Uncle Lou’s tonight at 9 p.m. tonight alongside Holding Cell, Deformed and Burn To Learn. Tickets are $7.
