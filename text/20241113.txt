DIY hub the Space Station shows off their new SoDo digs, soft-opening style
The usual litany of rising rents, landlords, gentrification — take your pick — forced DIY creative hub the Space Station out of its Coolidge Avenue location. But there’s a happy new chapter set to begin, thankfully ( rare these days! ).
The Station, a gallery space-slash-print shop-slash-music venue, has landed in SoDo with a new headquarters at 315 W. Grant St. , and they’re ready to show off their digs with a soft opening this weekend.
Pop by and peep the print shop and artist studios currently featuring the works of D.M. Hood and Aaron Sarkisian, and venture into the Attic to peruse vintage T-shirts. Mid Mod Urban Farm Bakehouse are peddling baked goods just for the occasion.
It’s worth your weekend time just to applaud their resilience in the face of continued adversity. Here’s to the next phase.
Friday and Saturday, Space Station, 315 W. Grant St., instagram.com/spacestationorlando, free.
