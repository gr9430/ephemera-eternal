Mr. Clit and the Pink Cigarettes play Uncle Lou’s tonight
Mr. Clit & the Pink Cigarettes (insert Ed McMahon’s “Hey oh” or Hank Kingsley’s “Hey now” as preferred), a hard-touring alien punkoid trio carves a swath through Florida at least once a year. Tonight they return to Uncle Lou’s.
Stacked bill of local support from Clem McGillicutty and the Burnouts, the Rot Guts, Warm Like Winter, Wayne Meadlo and All Points Bulletin. Come on feel the noise. Show is at 9 p.m. tonight, Cover is $5.
