Report: Drunken Florida man messes up pool table, throws ball through guitar
Have you heard the one about the drunk man, musician and pool players walking into a bar?
Well, this story is no joke, and it ends with a broken guitar, a ruined game and an arrest on Mills Avenue.
A musician was getting ready for his performance May 11 at Uncle Lou’s bar at 1016 N. Mills Ave.
Before his set, he put his $3,000 guitar on the stage as he made a last minute trip to the store, according to an arrest report.
Folks were playing pool in the bar, waiting for the show to begin. A bartender was serving drinks to patrons.
And that’s when 29-year-old Michael Lamar Columbus walked in, according to the report.
Columbus, of Orlando, started making a ruckus, smelled “strongly of alcohol” and the bartender said he was acting “very disorderly,” according to the report. Someone even had to ask him not to touch the guitar.
Instead, he walked up to a pool table where people are playing and “messed up all the balls on the table,” according to the report. The pool players, who were in the middle of a game, asked him to stop.
That’s when Columbus picked up a pool ball and threw it “directly at the guitar on stage, shattering the back of it,” according to the report.
The bartender told police she “felt that [Columbus] intentionally threw the ball at guitar with full intent on damaging it,” according to the report.
Columbus and a couple bar patrons then got in a scuffle, and they escorted him out of the bar, the report said.
After the commotion, the musician came back to the bar to find a “large” hole in his expensive instrument. He then called police and they arrested Columbus just north of the bar on charges of criminal mischief and disorderly intoxication.
Police said he was “acting very intoxicated” and appeared “to be extremely intoxicated.”
Columbus remains in the Orange County Jail on a $1,200 bond.
