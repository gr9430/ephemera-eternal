Hypercube Laboratories curate a diverse noise show at Uncle Lou’s tonight
Orlando Noise Night is a noise show reimagined through the rainbow prisms Hypercube Labs and Illuminated Paths – including IP-affiliated acts Ray Brazen, Broken Machine Films, Byson and Heliophonic. Plus Really Fast Horses. Added bonus: Brazen releases his newest album at this event.
Hypercube Laboratories Orlando Noise Night featuring Ray Brazen, Byson, Heliophonic, Really Fast Horses and Doktor Faux happens tonight at 9 p.m. at Uncle Lou’s. No cover.
