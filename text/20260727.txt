Jay Lucy’s will take over the Plantees space in Mills 50
A place that exclusively served plant-based burgers for more than four years is getting into the meat market. Last week, Team Market Group announced that their meatless burger concept, Plantees, was sold to a new owner who plans to move the brand to Arizona.
But in a couple of weeks, the group will rebrand the space into Jay Lucy’s , a joint specializing in grass-fed burgers, tallow fries and fun shakes. TMG executive chef Nick Grecco says Jay Lucy’s will griddle a steakhouse-style burger, but with a smashburger feel à la Au Cheval in New York City.
Burgers will be served on Olde Hearth brioche buns along with onions, pickles, cheese and a house koji miso sauce. Grecco says that beef tallow fries and “fun” shakes will also be offered, as will hand-battered nuggets and sauces.
The restaurant will also receive a design refresh with enclosed, air-conditioned seating areas keeping the sweat off your buns, while eclectic music and late-night hours ( ed. note: Surely not in competition with Will’s right next door, of course ) will lend the space a new vibe.
No doubt, more than a few herbivores will have a beef with Jay Lucy’s — though, if it’s any consolation, a plant-based burger will be offered.
