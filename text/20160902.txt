Miami freaks Bleeth and Period Bomb to play Uncle Lou’s tonight
We raved about Miami’s Period Bomb as a must-see when they last came through town a couple of months ago, and if you missed them then, you’ve got another chance tonight.
Period Bomb will be supporting fellow 305 residents and sludge-trawlers Bleeth – a monolithically heavy trio wondrously mired in Sabbath worship – when their tour hits (where else) Uncle Lou’s. Local-ish support is provided from noise enigma No Face , who is worth showing up early for, and Uh.
Show starts at 10-ish p,m. The cover is $4.
