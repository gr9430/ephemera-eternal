Shania Pain throw cassette release show tonight at Uncle Lou’s
An unlikely pairing – dreamwave factory Illuminated Paths and dark-pop prodigies Shania Pain – results in an impressive debut cassette release. Come see/hear the results tonight.
Shania Pain throw an album release show in the company of Ootheca, Harsh Radish, Byson, Broken Machine Films and Heliophonic at Uncle Lou’s tonight at 10 p.m. Cover is $5. Bring a few extra for the tape.
