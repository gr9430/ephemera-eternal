Florida Is Loud Fest to return to Orlando this December
Florida is Loud: The Sequel is a go. The DIY festival focusing on the various strains of Florida-related heaviness is back for another year and set for December.
Full lineup has been released and includes: Holly Hunt, Shroud Eater, Yashira, Gaul, Junior Bruce, Wørsen, ASM, Other Body, Computer, No Fraud, Forms, Los Reyesz Bong Death and many more.
Florida Is Loud takes place on Friday-Sunday, Dec. 15-17, at Will’s Pub and Uncle Lou’s Tickets are $10 for the Uncle Lou’s shows or $15-$18 to the Will’s Pub show. Daily lineups can be found on Facebook for Friday (Lou’s) – Saturday (Will’s) – and Sunday (Lou’s).
