Philadelphia indie-pop group Coping Skills to play Uncle Lou’s tonight
Coping Skills are a fully of-the-moment indie-pop outfit from Philly with a potent lyrical bite and a disregard for nostalgia. See for yourself tonight.
Coping Skills play a late show tonight at Uncle Lou’s joined by locals TV Dinner and Tallahassee’s Cooper. Show starts at 11 p.m. with a $5 cover.
