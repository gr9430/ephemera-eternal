Everyone we saw at one of the last shows at Uncle Lou’s last week
We’ve posted several of these, admittedly — but it’s important to preserve these last events at Uncle Lou’s for posterity’s sake. This one is particularly notable in that every artist featured either played crucial early shows here in their creative lives, or spent a good amount of time as both performer and audience. Or both! So Machine Gun Kristin, Future Bartenderz, S.M.O.P. and Warm Frames played their hearts out and people came to see and hear. Yes, this is all part of the end of an era.
