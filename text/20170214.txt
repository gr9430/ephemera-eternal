Bedroom pop group Tall Dog to play Uncle Lou’s tonight
A member of new Factory-influenced outfit RV steps out with a suite of elegant and lo-fi agoraphobic pop songs as Tall Dog.
Tall Dog supports Teen Divorce, Gray Smiths and Young Cries (every single one of those band names seem thematically perfect to play tonight) at 9 p.m. tonight at Uncle Lou’s. Cover is $5.
