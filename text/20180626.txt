New Jersey’s Pink Mass go over the top with S&M spectacle and sonic assault at Uncle Lou’s
Music fans in Orlando have clearly been very, very bad and are in need of another dose of punishment. Coming off like a queer, deadly serious Genitorturers or a sex-positive, leather-clad Repulsion, Pink Mass’ summer 2016 show at Uncle Lou’s was like Grand Guignol done on a DIY scale. Leather, sweat and intense S&M theatrics were soundtracked by unhinged grind-noise from this self-proclaimed “pansexual pervert punk” band. New Jersey’s Pink Mass are a band firing on all transgressive cylinders live, and that’s where it counts. FetLife meets Metal. And, as Judas Priest’s Rob Halford has ably demonstrated, it’s a winner for everyone involved.
